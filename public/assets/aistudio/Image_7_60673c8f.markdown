## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for an intelligent botanical diagnosis and plant vitality platform. The brand personality blends scientific precision with organic warmth: calm, deeply knowledgeable, encouraging, and clinical without feeling sterile. 

The aesthetic sits at the intersection of **Modern Organic Minimalism** and **Tactile Utility**:
- **Clarity over Clutter:** High-density botanical diagnosis metrics must remain legible under bright outdoor sunlight or dim greenhouse conditions.
- **Physical Connection:** Earthy neutrals offset digital precision, turning a smartphone into an ergonomic, modern field microscope and journal.
- **Visual Tone:** Composed, empathetic, and authoritative. Diagnosis results avoid panic-inducing medical metaphors, opting for clear diagnostic severity levels framed through natural transitions (amber dormancy to vibrant recovery).

## Colors

The color system relies on biological and ecological reference points:
- **Primary (`#134e43` - Deep Forest Spruce):** Grounds the interface with institutional authority, used for primary navigation, critical interactive states, structural framing, and primary text headers.
- **Secondary (`#10b981` - Vital Emerald):** Represents vigor, optimal health, confirmed matches, and affirmative actions. Used for success metrics, healthy status badges, and active scan rings.
- **Tertiary (`#f59e0b` - Sunlit Amber):** Acts as the primary warning tier for pest infestations, nutrient deficiencies, or hydration alerts. When escalated, a system coral (`#f43f5e`) handles irreversible tissue damage or terminal alerts.
- **Neutral (`#f4f6f0` - Soft Limestone):** An organic, desaturated tint replacing stark white canvas backgrounds. Softens eye fatigue in direct sun and provides a high-contrast backing for botanical photography.

Surface shifts use subtle tonal shifts from limestone to bleached parchment (`#ffffff`) for elevated diagnosis cards, ensuring high legibility and precise field identification.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear boundaries between botanical taxonomy, analytical telemetry, and narrative care instructions:
- **Headlines (Epilogue):** Editorial and sculptured. Provides a human, curated publication feel for plant common names, system summaries, and major diagnosis headers.
- **Body Text (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Highly legible, friendly grotesque. Accommodates multi-step treatment procedures, water schedules, and detailed pathology descriptions without visual fatigue.
- **Data & Telemetry (Space Grotesk):** Applied to binomial Latin nomenclature (e.g., *Monstera deliciosa*), sensor telemetry (lux, soil humidity percentage), match probabilities, and scan confidence metrics.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a strict mobile-first fluid grid structured around ergonomic single-hand interaction zones:
- **Layout Grid:** 
  - Mobile (0–599px): 4-column fluid grid, 16px margins, 16px gutters. Interactive elements cluster within the lower 60% viewport zone.
  - Tablet (600–1023px): 8-column fluid grid, 32px margins, 24px gutters. Dual-pane inspection view (specimen photo left, diagnostic panel right).
  - Desktop (1024px+): 12-column fixed container (max 1280px), 48px margins, 32px gutters.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows an 8pt spatial cadence with a strict 4pt sub-grid for badge typography and tightly coupled sensor indicators.
- **Ergonomics:** Safe area insets respect hardware camera cutouts and bottom gesture bars. Diagnostic capture viewports float UI controls 24px above system home indicators.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects exaggerated drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Layers** combined with **Low-Contrast Organic Outlines**:
- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** Base background surface in `#f4f6f0`.
- **Layer 1 (Card Default):** Crisp `#ffffff` container bordered by a delicate 1px stroke of tinted slate-forest (`rgba(19, 78, 67, 0.08)`). No drop shadow.
- **Layer 2 (Elevated & Active Overlays):** Used for diagnosis bottom sheets, drawer panels, and active tooltips. Features a soft, organic ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(19, 78, 67, 0.12)`.
- **Camera Viewfinder Scrim:** Dynamic dark veil (`rgba(10, 28, 24, 0.65)`) with an integrated frosted blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) to keep scanning controls legible when pointed against high-glare foliage.

## Shapes

The interface balances biological curvature with scientific structure:
- **Base Corner Radius (`roundedness: 2`):** Primary interactive elements, cards, and input fields utilize an 8px (`0.5rem`) radius.
- **Large Elements (`rounded-lg`):** Diagnosis sheets, modal dialogs, and photographic comparison frames expand to 16px (`1rem`).
- **Interactive Tokens & Badges (`rounded-xl` / Full):** Filter chips, severity tags, and primary capture triggers use 24px (`1.5rem`) up to full pill shapes to indicate immediate tap affordance and soft tactile responsiveness.

## Components

### Buttons & Capture Controls
- **Primary Action (Diagnose / Scan):** Forest green fill (`#134e43`) with white text, 52px height for thumb targeting, pill radius, paired with Space Grotesk semi-bold labels. On active press: scale down to 0.98 with subtle brightness reduction.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent base with a 1.5px solid border in `#134e43`, text tinted to match.
- **Floating Camera Shutter:** 72px dual-ring button; outer ring in vital emerald (`#10b981`), white core, 4px internal spacer.

### Diagnosis & Specimen Cards
- **Structure:** Surface white background, 12px internal padding, 8px radius, subtle border.
- **Card Header:** Epilogue headline for common plant name, Space Grotesk label for Latin genus, right-aligned severity indicator.
- **Photographic Split:** High-aspect 1:1 image mask with rounded internal corners showing afflicted vs. healthy sample references.

### Severity & Status Chips
- **Low Severity (Healthy / Resolved):** Soft emerald background (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`), solid emerald text (`#047857`), circle status dot.
- **Medium Severity (Action Needed / Dehydrated):** Light amber background (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.14)`), dark amber text (`#b45309`).
- **Critical Severity (Fungal / Pest Threat):** Coral background (`rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.14)`), deep crimson-coral text (`#be123c`).

### Inputs & Botanical Filters
- **Search & Input Fields:** 48px height, 8px radius, light cream fill (`#ecefe6`), resting border `rgba(19, 78, 67, 0.12)`. Focus shifts border to emerald (`#10b981`) with 2px outer glow.
- **Selection Pills:** Horizontal scrollable row, unselected state in soft limestone, selected state in filled forest green with white text.

### Telemetry & Vital Gauges
- Specialized readouts for Sunlight, Soil Moisture, and Ambient Temperature: segmented linear bars with rounded ends (4px height) displaying safe vs. danger thresholds via gradient transitions.
